Codex Workspace

Codex Workspace is a personal OAuth client used by its owner to connect a locally running AI assistant (Codex) to the owner's own Google account.

It allows the owner to read, organize, draft and send email in their own Gmail account, and to browse, upload and manage files in their own Google Drive, through the official Gmail API and Google Drive API.

This application is used by a single person (its owner) only. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it does not collect data from anyone else.
